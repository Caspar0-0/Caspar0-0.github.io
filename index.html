02 — Platform

The warehouse behind the answer.

A terabyte-scale Snowflake warehouse. Staging, intermediate, and marts, in dbt, for Finance, Marketing, Operations, and executive reporting. Ask Taskrabbit is held to the same names as the dashboards.

  1. 30%+

    Snowflake compute, reduced 30%+ by query profiling, incremental modeling, partition pruning, clustering, and reusable SQL and Jinja.

  2. 40%

    Mean incident response time, reduced 40%. Snowflake governance in Terraform: roles and grants, dynamic data masking, column tagging, automated tests, and quality alerting.

  3. 160+

    Production tables under schema contracts. Ingestion from Stripe, Fixer, Segment, Attom, S3, and internal microservices, through Fivetran and custom Python connectors.

Dev/Staging/Prod Releases

Isolated dev, staging, and prod, with role-based access and a controlled promotion. Staging runs changed models and what depends on them in dbt Cloud CI, with modified+ and --fail-fast, before promotion. Scheduled jobs release through Jenkins, Ansible, and cron.

Airflow on Astronomer.

Dynamic task mapping, cross-DAG dependencies, resilient retries, and daily runs over millions of marketplace events.

Snowflake Streams and Tasks.
