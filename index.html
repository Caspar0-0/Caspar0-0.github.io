01 — Work

The question has a contract.

Ask Taskrabbit

Ask Taskrabbit is a company-wide, Claude-based text-to-SQL product. Employees query governed company metrics in natural language and receive consistent, dashboard-aligned answers at approved grains and dimensions.

Claude connects to dbt through MCP, and to Snowflake through a least-privilege service role scoped to approved databases and schemas.

The dbt Semantic Layer and the LLM metric guardrails are the canonical KPI contract. They standardize metric names, grain and dimensions, aggregation-safe ratio formulas — IBR, AOV, Take Rate, QFR — and LY/WoW comparison logic, so AI-generated SQL follows the business definitions used in production dashboards.
